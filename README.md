# Power BI Project

AI Impact on Jobs 2030: Workforce Vulnerability & Upskilling Outlook

Project Overview:
This project presents an end-to-end interactive Microsoft Power BI dashboard suite analyzing the transformative impact of Artificial Intelligence on global employment markets heading into 2030. Using advanced DAX modeling, multi-page visual layout design, and statistical data breakdown, the report evaluates workforce risk exposure, compensation patterns, demand scores, and targeted mitigation strategies. 

Dataset Details & Source:
•	Dataset Name: AI_Impact_on_Jobs_2030.csv

•	Source Platform: Kaggle

•	Data Volume & Structure: 773 records across 20 attributes, modeled into a star-schema structure with Fact_Employees and a normalized Bridge_Skills dimension table.

•	Key Attributes Analyzed: AI_Replacement_Risk, Job_Growth_2030, Average_Salary_USD, Future_Demand_Score, Automation_Level, Upskilling_Needed, AI_Tool_Usage, Performance_Score, and Education_Level. 

Core Project Goals:
•	Quantify global AI replacement risk across industries, education levels, and company sizes.

•	Map high-risk roles against projected job growth and compensation to identify vulnerable sectors.

•	Provide concrete upskilling and AI adoption insights that enable policy makers and HR leaders to build workforce resilience. 

Key Findings & Results:
•	Widespread Exposure: Average AI replacement risk across all roles sits at 51.16%, with 49.68% of the workforce requiring immediate upskilling. 

•	Education Vulnerability: Highest upskilling necessity occurs at the PhD level (54.55%), demonstrating that advanced specialization does not guarantee immunity from AI automation. 

•	AI Tool Synergy: Employees actively utilizing AI tools at Moderate to High levels consistently achieve higher average performance scores (3.54 - 3.55 / 5.0) compared to Low-usage peers (3.45 / 5.0).

In conclusion, while over 51% of analyzed positions face significant AI replacement risk by 2030, automation threats are heavily offset by upskilling programs and tool adoption. Proactive training in high-demand roles transforms automation risk into productivity gains, establishing long-term workforce sustainability.
